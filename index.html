01

ORVIX – Smart Crop. Healthy Harvest

EfficientNetB0 · TensorFlow Lite · Offline AI · Multilingual Chatbot

ORVIX (Observation, Research, Vision, Intelligence & Next Generation) is an offline AI-powered crop disease detection and farmer advisory solution that makes intelligent agricultural help accessible even without internet connectivity.

  • Scan a leaf: capture a leaf image with the camera or pick one from the gallery.
  • AI disease detection: an EfficientNetB0-based TensorFlow Lite model runs on the device and identifies 38 disease classes with no internet.
  • Treatment guidance: locally stored symptoms, remedies, chemical treatments, severity, treatment costs and a structured action plan.
  • Offline AI chatbot: answers crop-health questions from a local agricultural knowledge base in English, Tamil and Hindi.
  • Offline reports & history: generates diagnostic reports and keeps a local scan history.
